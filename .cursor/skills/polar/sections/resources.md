# Pricing

This section provides an overview of the pricing structure for Polar, including transaction fees, additional fees, examples, and information on refunds, disputes, chargebacks, and payout fees.

## Introduction

### Cheapest MoR on the Market

Polar is priced 20% cheaper than other Merchant of Record (MoR) alternatives, with a standard fee of 4% plus 40¢ per transaction.

### No Hidden Fees

Polar does not impose any extra fees or markups. However, payouts may incur fees from payout providers like Stripe.

## Transaction Fees

### Standard Fees

Polar charges a fee of 4% plus 40¢ per transaction. This fee covers the 2.9% plus 30¢ fee charged by Stripe, along with additional fees for certain transactions.

## Additional Fees

- **International Cards (non-US)**: +1.5%
- **Subscription Payments**: +0.5%

Polar reserves the right to pass on any future fees imposed by Stripe.

## Example

Here’s a breakdown of fees for a $30 subscription payment from Sweden (25% VAT):

| Description                      | Amount  |
|----------------------------------|---------|
| Product Price                    | $30     |
| VAT (25%)                        | $7.50   |
| Total Transaction Value           | $37.50  |
| Transaction Fee (4% + 40¢)      | $1.90   |
| International Card (+1.5%)      | $0.56   |
| Subscription (+0.5%)            | $0.19   |
| **Total Fees (Before Payout)**  | **$2.65** |

## Refunds

Polar allows both full and partial refunds. However, initial transaction fees are not refundable due to charges from credit card networks and payment service providers (PSPs). 

Polar reserves the right to issue refunds at its discretion within 60 days of purchase to reduce disputes and chargebacks, which incur a fee of $15 per dispute.

## Dispute/Chargeback Fees

Customers may initiate disputes or chargebacks through their banks, resulting in a fee of $15 per dispute, deducted directly from your balance. This fee is non-refundable and is charged by credit card networks and PSPs.

Polar actively monitors chargeback rates to maintain them below industry standards. Accounts may be suspended if chargeback rates exceed acceptable thresholds.

## Payout Fees

Payouts may incur fees from payout providers like Stripe, but Polar does not add any extra fees or markups. 

### Stripe Fees

- **Monthly Fee**: $2 per month for active payouts
- **Per Payout Fee**: 0.25% + $0.25

#### Cross-Border Fees (Currency Conversion)

- **EU**: 0.25%
- **Other Countries**: 1%
